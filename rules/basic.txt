BAN LIST

The following words, phrases, constructions, and concepts are strictly forbidden in narration.

The rules below are absolute unless an explicit exception is stated.

Do not evade a ban by replacing the forbidden wording with a direct synonym, near-synonym, paraphrase, or an equivalent cliché.

Do not quantify interpersonal distance for dramatic effect.
Use spatial relationships naturally: рядом, почти вплотную, в шаге от неё, перед ней, слишком близко etc.
Exact measurements are appropriate only when the measurement itself matters to the character or action.

When a banned expression would naturally occur, reconstruct the moment from the underlying event instead of searching for a substitute phrase.


FORBIDDEN WORDS

The following words and their grammatical forms are forbidden:

- якорь — unless referring to a ship's physical anchor
- озон
- мускус
- сандал
- хищник
- хищника
- хищнику
- хищники
- хищный
- хищно
- звериный
- зверь
- одержимость
- одержимый
- собственник
- собственничество
- собственнически
- животный
- властно
- властный


FORBIDDEN SENSATIONS AND PHRASES

Do not use:

- металлический привкус when describing blood, fear, or emotion
- медное послевкусие
- прижался лбом к ее лбу


FORBIDDEN SOUNDS

Characters must not:

- growl like an animal
- roar like an animal
- purr like an animal

Do not use animal vocalizations as shorthand for dominance, attraction, anger, possessiveness, or sexual tension.


FORBIDDEN CLICHÉS

Do not use these phrases or their obvious equivalents:

- "X-D / 2D / 3D / etc. шахматы"
- "Воздух был густым"
- "Воздух был неподвижным"
- "Воздух был тяжёлым"
- similar descriptions of air whose only purpose is to intensify atmosphere
- "Это было капитуляцией"
- "Его/её голос треснул"
- "Что-то, чему он/она не давала имени"
- "Хирургическая точность"
- "Тишина, которую можно резать ножом"
- "Голос упал до интимного шёпота"
- "Он целовал ее так, словно хотел выпить весь кислород из ее легких"
- "сокращая дистанцию до опасного минимума"
- "голос сломался на втором/третьем/любом слоге"
- "Не потому... А потому..."
- "Впервые в жизни" and equivalent grandiose variants
- "Заявить о своих правах" in any context
- "голос как рокот"
- descriptions of body movement using arbitrary degree measurements such as "плечи сдвинулись на два градуса"
- robotic numerical measurements of interpersonal distance or body positioning
- "ровным, лишенным всяких эмоций голосом"


Do not recreate these clichés through superficial paraphrase.

If the underlying event is simple, describe the simple event.


<negative_reframing>

ABSOLUTE RHETORICAL BAN

Do not use negative reframing as a literary device.

Negative reframing means introducing an interpretation by rejecting, correcting, downgrading, or dismissing X and then presenting Y as the deeper, truer, more accurate, or more significant interpretation.

The ban applies across wording, punctuation, sentence boundaries, conjunctions, and paraphrases.

The forbidden rhetorical function is:

reject X → replace X with Y → imply that Y is the real or deeper meaning.

Forbidden patterns include:

- "Not X, but Y."
- "Not X. Y."
- "It wasn't X. It was Y."
- "He didn't X. He Y." when the construction exists primarily to create rhetorical contrast
- "X wasn't the point. Y was."
- "Not because X. Because Y."
- "It wasn't really X; it was Y."
- equivalent constructions using "instead", "rather", "only", "just", or similar wording when they perform the same rhetorical function

Do not evade this ban by:

- changing punctuation;
- splitting one sentence into several;
- replacing the connective with a synonym;
- using a dash;
- using a semicolon;
- turning the contrast into a short fragment;
- moving the same contrast into internal narration.


IMPORTANT:

This is NOT a ban on ordinary negation.

Normal negation remains valid when it communicates:

- factual absence;
- refusal;
- inability;
- uncertainty;
- failed action;
- contradiction;
- denial;
- limitation;
- ordinary comparison;
- character thought;
- ordinary negative description.

Do not rewrite ordinary negative statements merely because they contain:

- not;
- didn't;
- never;
- without;
- no;
- nothing.

The problem is the rhetorical "X is rejected so Y can be revealed as the deeper truth", not negation itself.


REPLACEMENT

When negative reframing would normally appear, state the relevant perception, action, thought, or consequence directly.

Prefer:

- direct description;
- concrete action;
- specific perception;
- character-grounded interpretation;
- causal explanation;
- observable consequence.

Do not replace negative reframing with another decorative rhetorical device.

Do not manufacture a new metaphor merely to preserve the dramatic effect.


</negative_reframing>


<forbidden_emotional_constructions>

Do not use the following artificial emotional patterns:

1. Negation + gradation

Forbidden:

"Внутри него что-то разбилось. Не сломалось, не треснуло. Разбилось."

"Это был не страх, нет, не тревога — это была тоска."

2. Vague internal object + pseudo-poetic action

Avoid constructions such as:

"Внутри неё что-то оборвалось."

"Внутри него что-то сжалось."

"Внутри неё что-то замерло."

"Внутри него что-то перевернулось."

when the "something" has no concrete referent and exists only to dramatize an emotion.

3. Layered abstract emotional definitions

Avoid:

"Не боль, а пустота."

"Не злость, а усталость."

"Не страх, а что-то глубже."

Do not replace these constructions with another abstract rhetorical contrast.

Describe what the character actually thinks, does, notices, says, or experiences.


</forbidden_emotional_constructions>


<selective_perception>

Characters perceive selectively according to:

- attention;
- personality;
- knowledge;
- situation;
- immediate goals;
- emotional state;
- current action.

Do not portray ordinary perception as an inventory, checklist, database, audit, systematic scan, or environmental report.

Avoid unnecessary:

- counting;
- enumeration;
- cataloguing;
- ranking;
- tallying;
- sorting;
- repeated checking;
- environmental inventories;
- mental lists of objects;
- lists of people;
- lists of exits;
- lists of sensations;
- lists of injuries;
- lists of risks;
- lists of options;
- lists of reactions.

Such behavior is allowed when the immediate task genuinely requires it, for example:

- professional work;
- combat;
- medical procedure;
- investigation;
- puzzle solving;
- navigation;
- established character habit;
- deliberate planning.

Otherwise, select the few details that matter to the viewpoint character now.

Do not replace an obvious list with disguised cataloguing such as:

- "taking stock";
- "tracking everything";
- "registering each detail";
- "mentally filing";
- "noting one after another";
- similar pseudo-natural inventory language.

Perception should feel selective and human, not comprehensive.


</selective_perception>


<replacement_principle>

When a banned phrase, cliché, or artificial construction would naturally occur, do NOT search for a stock replacement phrase.

Reconstruct the moment from the underlying event.

Ask:

- What physically happened?
- What did the viewpoint character actually perceive?
- What changed?
- What action caused it?
- What concrete detail matters right now?
- What would THIS character naturally notice?
- What consequence follows from the event?
- What does the character understand or misunderstand?

Write the answer directly in the established viewpoint and vocabulary.

Do not replace one cliché with another cliché.

Do not turn a forbidden metaphor into a more elaborate metaphor.

Do not turn every emotion into a physical reaction.

Do not automatically add sensory detail.

Do not expand a simple event merely because the original phrase was banned.

Specificity is preferred over ornament.

Character relevance is preferred over generic sensory detail.

Direct description is preferred over literary substitution.

Concrete evidence is preferred over decorative explanation.
